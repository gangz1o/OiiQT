# OiiQT 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

本文档为中英文对照版本。
This document is provided in both Chinese and English.

## 1. 数据收集与使用 / Data Collection & Use

中文：OiiQT 是一款纯粹的本地客户端工具。我们不收集、不上传、不共享您的服务器地址、用户名、密码或任何下载任务信息。所有敏感信息均加密存储在您设备的本地存储或 iOS 钥匙串（Keychain）中。

English: OiiQT is a pure local client tool. We do not collect, upload, or share your server addresses, usernames, passwords, or any download task information. All sensitive information is stored encrypted locally on your device or within the iOS Keychain.

## 2. 第三方服务 / Third-Party Services

中文：为了提供丰富的影院级体验，本应用集成以下服务：

- TMDB：用于获取影视海报及元数据。
- Kingfisher：用于海报图片的本地缓存优化。

这些服务可能会根据其自身的隐私政策处理您的公开 IP 地址以获取媒体资源。

English: To provide a cinematic experience, this app integrates the following services:

- TMDB: Used to retrieve movie posters and metadata.
- Kingfisher: Used for local image caching and optimization.

These services may process your public IP address to deliver media assets according to their respective privacy policies.

## 3. 数据安全 / Data Security

中文：我们利用 iOS 的系统级安全特性保护您的配置。当您删除应用时，所有本地存储的相关配置信息将同步被抹除。

English: We utilize iOS system-level security features to protect your configurations. When you delete the application, all locally stored configuration information will be erased simultaneously.
